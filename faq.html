FAQ

Are these calculators accurate?

Our calculators use standard industry formulas and provide reliable estimates. However, actual loan terms may vary by lender and your specific credit profile.

Is my financial data stored?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We never collect or store your financial information.

Do you sell loans?

No. We are an educational resource only. We don't originate, broker, or sell any financial products.

Who writes the articles?

Articles are written by Marcus Cole, a former bank loan officer with 15 years of industry experience.
