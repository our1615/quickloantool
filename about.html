About QuickLoanTool.org

Real tools from someone who sat on the other side of the desk for 15 years.

MT

Marcus Cole

Former senior loan officer at two of Atlanta's biggest banks. 15 years in lending before walking away to help regular people understand how loans really work. MBA in Finance from Georgia State University (2012). NMLS-licensed loan officer (#1423785). Living in Decatur with wife Keisha and coaching Little League.

Why This Site Exists

I spent my career watching good people get bad loans because they didn't understand the system. QuickLoanTool.org gives you the same calculators and insights bankers use — without the sales pitch.

Trust & Independence

I don't sell loans, insurance, or financial products. This site is supported by advertising and affiliate links, clearly marked. My only agenda is helping you make smarter borrowing decisions.
